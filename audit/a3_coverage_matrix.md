## Build order recommendation

1. Build `eco114_a3_exam_warroom.html` source-driven tools first: formula audit, graph audit, T/F traps, MCQ traps and long-answer templates.
2. Build Unit 7 and Unit 8 market/calculation cards next because they are the clearest marks-heavy calculation zone.
3. Build Unit 10 policy/market-failure cards next because they integrate welfare, graphs and policy reasoning.
4. Build Unit 6 labour discipline and employment rent carefully, with a comparison to Unit 8 minimum wage logic.
5. Build Units 1-5 foundations last as a connected concept system, not as isolated summaries.
