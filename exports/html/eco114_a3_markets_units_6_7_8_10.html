Markets 80%+ minimum mastery checklist















Unit 8 Deep Pack: Supply, Demand And Competitive Markets

How to use Unit 8

First pass: learn demand/supply and equilibrium. Second pass: do price controls and taxes. Third pass: redraw surplus, wedge and DWL graphs. Final pass: answer practice without notes.

Most examinable patterns

Qd=Qs, binding ceiling/floor, shortage/surplus, tax revenue at post-tax quantity, DWL triangle, incidence by elasticity, and long-run zero economic profit.

80%+ Unit 8 checklist




Unit 7 Deep Pack: The Firm And Its Customers

How to use Unit 7

First pass: convert demand and derive MR. Second pass: solve MR=MC and read price from demand. Third pass: practise profit rectangles and elasticity advice. Final pass: explain why P greater than MC creates DWL.

Most examinable patterns

Inverse demand, TR/MR, MR double-slope rule, MR=MC, price from demand, profit=(P-AC)Q, elasticity versus total revenue, and monopoly DWL.

80%+ Unit 7 checklist




Unit 10 Deep Pack: Market Successes And Failures

How to use Unit 10

First pass: separate private and social curves. Second pass: calculate Qm and Q*. Third pass: attach the policy instrument: tax, subsidy, regulation or bargaining. Final pass: explain limits such as transaction costs and information problems.

Most examinable patterns

Negative externality overproduction, positive externality underproduction, Pigouvian tax/subsidy at Q*, public-good classification, Coase conditions, moral hazard and adverse selection.

80%+ Unit 10 checklist




Unit 6 Deep Pack: Firm And Employees

How to use Unit 6

First pass: understand incomplete contracts and effort incentives. Second pass: calculate employment rent using one time unit. Third pass: redraw best response and isocost logic. Final pass: compare Unit 6 minimum wage logic with Unit 8 competitive labour markets.

Most examinable patterns

Employment rent, reservation option, wage above reservation, labour discipline, involuntary unemployment, no-shirking wage, and w/e versus e/w.

80%+ Unit 6 checklist

Calculation Emergency Room

Start any calculation

Write the model name, formula, substitution, answer and one interpretation sentence.

Keep units consistent

Convert hourly, weekly and duration values before multiplying employment rent.

Do not read price from MR

For Unit 7, MR=MC gives Q. Demand gives P.

Binding controls

Ceiling binds below equilibrium; floor binds above equilibrium.

Tax side shift

Seller tax raises supply by tax; buyer tax lowers demand by tax. Wedge result is equivalent.

Pigouvian Q*

Find social optimum first, then evaluate MEC or MEB at Q*.
