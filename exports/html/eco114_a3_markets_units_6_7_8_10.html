Source And Scope Warning

A3 is treated as the full-semester assessment covering Units 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 10. Unit 9 is excluded unless the lecturer later confirms it is examinable. This page is a structure-only scaffold for the detailed build.

Page Control Centre

Purpose

Marks-heavy models, calculations and graphs for Units 6, 7, 8 and 10.

Emphasis

Graph drawing, formulas, application questions, and policy analysis.

Output Later

Detailed model cards, SVG diagrams, and practice banks will be added in the content build.

Unit 6 - Firm And Employees

Scaffold slot for firm hierarchy, authority, reservation wage, employment rent, best-response effort, no-shirking wage, isocost/isoprofit logic, labour discipline, and involuntary unemployment.

Unit 7 - Price-Setting Firms

Scaffold slot for differentiated demand, MR, MC, AC, MR = MC, price from demand, monopoly profit, markup, elasticity, total revenue, and deadweight loss.

Unit 8 - Competitive Markets

Scaffold slot for demand, supply, competitive equilibrium, CS, PS, total surplus, taxes, incidence, DWL, price ceilings, price floors, shortages, and surpluses.

Unit 10 - Market Failures And Public Policy

Scaffold slot for externalities, MPC/MEC/MSC, MPB/MEB/MSB, Pigouvian taxes/subsidies, public goods, common resources, Coase bargaining, moral hazard, and adverse selection.

Calculation Slots

Calculation coverage will include equilibrium, shifts, tax revenue, tax incidence, DWL, surplus, MR, profit, elasticity, employment rent, efficiency units, MSC, MSB, Pigouvian policy, and Gini/Lorenz where relevant.
