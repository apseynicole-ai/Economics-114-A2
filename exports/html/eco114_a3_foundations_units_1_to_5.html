Purpose

Foundations and theory hub for Units 1 to 5.

Emphasis

Concept precision, diagrams, game theory, fairness, and constrained choice.

Output Later

Detailed cards, diagrams, traps, and exam responses will be added in the content build.

Unit 1 - Prosperity, Inequality And Capitalism

Scaffold slot for prosperity, inequality, planetary limits, GDP, PPP, capitalism, measurement, and long-run change.

Unit 2 - Technology, Incentives And Production

Scaffold slot for technology, incentives, opportunity cost, economic rent, production functions, feasible choices, and isocost reasoning.

Unit 3 - Scarcity, Wellbeing And Working Hours

Scaffold slot for scarcity, wellbeing, feasible set, indifference curves, MRS, MRT, constrained choice, and working-hours applications.

Unit 4 - Strategic Interactions And Social Dilemmas

Scaffold slot for payoff matrices, best responses, Nash equilibrium, dominant strategies, prisoner’s dilemma, public goods, social dilemmas, and repeated games.

Unit 5 - Institutions, Power, Pareto And Fairness

Scaffold slot for Angela-Bruno models, institutions, bargaining, Pareto efficiency, fairness, reservation options, forced labour, and take-it-or-leave-it contracts.
