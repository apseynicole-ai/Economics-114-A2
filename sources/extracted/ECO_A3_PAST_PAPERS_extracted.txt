This document is a comprehensive undergraduate economics examination paper designed to assess a student's grasp of both microeconomic theory and broader socioeconomic trends. The assessment is divided into three distinct sections: objective true/false statements, multiple-choice questions exploring core concepts like capitalism and market failure, and detailed descriptive problems requiring mathematical calculations. Key themes woven throughout the text include individual decision-making under constraints, the strategic interactions of game theory, and the mechanics of market equilibrium involving taxes, subsidies, and monopolies. Ultimately, the purpose of this source is to evaluate a learner's ability to apply formal economic modeling to real-world scenarios, ranging from historical Malthusian traps to modern environmental externalities.
